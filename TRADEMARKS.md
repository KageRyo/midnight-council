# Trademark and brand policy

This policy separates the Midnight Council project's code license from its
name and visual identity. It describes the project's current brand-use
expectations and does not replace rights that may exist under applicable law.

## Project marks

The project marks include:

- `Midnight Council`
- `MidnightCouncil`
- official logos, icons, artwork, and other visual branding
- translations, transliterations, localized renderings, and confusingly
  similar names presented as the project or an official offering, including
  examples such as `午夜議會` and `ミッドナイト・カウンシル`

This list is illustrative rather than exhaustive.

## Code and brand are separate

The source code is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. That
license permits reuse, modification, and redistribution under its terms, but
it does not grant permission to use the project's trade names, trademarks,
service marks, or product names except as allowed by the license and
applicable law.

## Forks and permitted references

Forks and derivative distributions should choose a distinct name, logo, and
visual identity. Factual references are allowed when they do not suggest
official sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation. Examples include:

- `Based on Midnight Council`
- `Fork of Midnight Council`
- `Compatible with the Midnight Council protocol`

These references should make the relationship clear and should not make the
fork appear to be an official Midnight Council release.

## Uses that require permission

Without prior written permission, do not use the project marks or confusingly
similar translations/localizations:

- as the primary name of a fork, product, hosted service, package, or
  commercial offering;
- in names such as `Midnight Council Taiwan` or `Midnight Council Pro`;
- with official-looking logos, icons, artwork, or other presentation; or
- in a way that implies the project maintains, endorses, or sponsors the
  resulting software or service.

No permission to use the project marks is granted by `LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md`, or
the availability of the source code. Permission, if granted, should be
obtained separately and in writing.
